Blaster TV UK Refund Policy — IPTV UK Subscription

The 24-hour trial is there to help you test compatibility before paying. This page explains the refund approach after purchase.

Try first

Please use the 24-hour trial on your intended device and broadband before purchasing. It is the clearest way to check compatibility and channel priorities.

When to contact us

If a paid service does not activate or a verified technical fault prevents access, contact Blaster TV UK promptly with your device, app and error details so support can investigate. Please give the team a reasonable chance to fix the problem before asking for a refund.

Change-of-mind requests

Digital services may begin immediately, which can affect cancellation rights. Any refund request will be considered under the agreed terms and applicable UK consumer law. A refund is not guaranteed where the service was delivered as described and the issue comes from the customer’s own device, broadband or a change of mind.

Partial use

Where part of a fixed-term plan has been used, any refund will take that use into account. Each request is reviewed on its own facts.

How to request a review

Use the contact page and include the purchase date, plan and a clear description of the issue. Do not send card details or passwords.
