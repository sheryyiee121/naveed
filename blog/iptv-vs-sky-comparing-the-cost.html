IPTV vs Sky: Comparing the Cost

Compare the full household bill, not just the headline monthly figure.

Updated 14 October 2026·6 minute read

Compare the full household bill, not just the headline monthly figure.

List the whole Sky bill

Include entertainment, sport, cinema, multi-room, hardware and any broadband bundle effects. The headline monthly figure is rarely the whole story.

Dig out a recent bill or check your account. Add-ons that felt small at sign-up often add up to a meaningful amount across a year.

Compare like with like

Check which channels, on-demand features, recording tools and simultaneous screens you actually use. A cheaper service that drops the channels you watch most is not a saving.

Write down the five things your household actually watches each week. Compare services against that list, not against a channel count you will never open.

Allow for equipment

An external streaming device or faster broadband can add to the first-year cost. A Firestick is inexpensive, but it is still a cost, and so is any broadband upgrade you need.

On the other side, leaving a bundled TV package can change broadband pricing. Check what your broadband costs on its own before assuming the saving.

Keep rights and reliability in view

Price is only one part of value. Legal access, support and service stability should carry real weight in the decision.

A service with a trial, clear terms and a real support channel is easier to trust than one that is cheap and anonymous. The cheapest option that stops working in a month is the most expensive one.

A fair way to decide

Add up your current yearly TV spend, then compare it with a Blaster TV UK plan for the same period. The home page calculator can help you see the shape of the difference.

Then try the trial. A cost comparison only matters if the service actually suits your household, and the trial is how you find out.
