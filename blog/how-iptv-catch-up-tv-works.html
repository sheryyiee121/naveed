How IPTV Catch-Up TV Works

Understand replay windows, availability and the controls to look for.

Updated 14 October 2026·6 minute read

Understand replay windows, availability and the controls to look for.

Not every channel includes catch-up

Replay depends on the source and rights. A clock or replay icon normally marks available programmes, and channels without one simply do not offer it.

Do not assume a service has catch-up everywhere. Check the channel list or ask before buying if replay matters to you.

Open the guide backwards

Choose a previous date and programme in the EPG. If replay is available, the player should offer a play option.

The guide is the usual route. Some players also have a dedicated catch-up section, but the backwards guide is the most common way to find a missed programme.

Know the window

Catch-up may last a day, several days or not be offered at all. Check the service details rather than assuming.

A programme that was available yesterday may be gone today. Windows vary by channel and by rights, so treat anything you really want to keep as something to watch promptly.

Troubleshoot missing replays

Refresh the guide, confirm the programme has finished and try another known catch-up channel. A programme that is still broadcasting is not yet available to replay.

If catch-up fails on every channel, restart the player and check your connection. If it fails on one channel only, report the channel and programme time to support.

Catch-up versus recording

Catch-up is not the same as recording. You cannot choose what gets kept, and you cannot extend the window. If you want to keep something, watch it inside the window.

Some players offer recording where the device supports it, but that depends on the player and the service. Ask before assuming it works.
