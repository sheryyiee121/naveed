Who it suits

Who this plan is for.

The 3-month plan suits households that have done the trial and want a proper run without a full-year decision. It covers a football season’s opening stretch, a run of box sets, or three months of family viewing — long enough to know the service, short enough to keep your options open.

It is also a good fit if your household watches in phases: a big sporting season, the darker winter evenings, or a stretch when everyone is home. You lock in a lower monthly rate than the 1-month plan, but you are only ever three months in.
